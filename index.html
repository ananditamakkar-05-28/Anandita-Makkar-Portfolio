Manuscript in preparation

HER2 MHC Class II Peptide–Pulsed DC1 Prime CD4+ Th1 Cells That Target Disseminated Cancer Cells in Breast Cancer

Anandita Makkar · Brian Czerniecki Laboratory, H. Lee Moffitt Cancer Center

Abstract

Intratumoral priming of HER2-specific CD4+ Th1 cells with HER2 MHC class II peptide–pulsed type 1 dendritic cells (HER2-DC1) eliminates disseminated cancer cells (DCCs) in preclinical breast cancer models and reduces metastasis-initiating cell burden in early patient data. DCCs in the bone marrow predict late recurrence and metastasis, yet conventional therapies often fail to eradicate them. Computational tools predict MHC class II epitopes poorly, so the laboratory identifies immunogenic class II peptides experimentally using a DC1 screening platform.

DC1 pulsed with HER2/neu peptides p5, p435 and p1209 and delivered into the mammary gland of BALB-neuT mice delayed spontaneous tumour growth and prevented micrometastasis to the lung, liver and brain. The effect required CD4+ T cells and IFN-γ and was independent of CD8+ T cells. Remaining DCCs showed increased senescence and failed to form tumours in immunodeficient mice for up to 120 days. HER3 peptide–pulsed DC1 reproduced the effect in triple-negative breast cancer and melanoma models, and in a clinical case series intratumoral DC1 reduced disseminating tumour-cell phenotypes and the bone marrow burden of metastasis-initiating cells in patients with stage I–III HER2+ disease.

Together, these findings identify antitumour CD4+ Th1 immunity as an immune mechanism that controls DCC-driven recurrence and metastasis.

Upcoming paper

Structure-Based Virtual Screening of FDA-Approved Drugs Against Dipeptidyl Peptidase-4 for Type 2 Diabetes: A Drug Repurposing Approach

Anandita Makkar · University of South Florida

Abstract

Type 2 diabetes affects hundreds of millions of people worldwide. Dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) degrades incretin hormones that stimulate insulin secretion, making it a validated drug target. This paper presents the workflow and results of docking 100 ZINC compounds structurally similar (≥50%) to approved gliptins into human DPP-4 (PDB 4A5S) using AutoDock Vina.

Re-docking the co-crystal ligand reproduced its crystal pose (RMSD 0.4 Å), and the scores separated known actives from decoys (ROC AUC 0.918). Thirty compounds scored between −8 and −10.8 kcal/mol. The best, ZINC000003015356 (−10.8 kcal/mol), outscored the co-crystal ligand (−10.5 kcal/mol), and a 200 ns molecular dynamics simulation supported its stable binding. The top hit did not bind DPP-8 or DPP-9 in docking.

  • 100 ZINC compounds
  • PDB 4A5S
  • AutoDock Vina
  • re-dock RMSD 0.4 Å
  • ROC AUC 0.918
  • top hit −10.8 kcal/mol
  • 200 ns MD
  • MM/PBSA −151.1 ± 11.2 kJ/mol

These are computational predictions, not proof of inhibition. No compound has been tested in the lab, and the top hit warrants enzyme assays.
